How do I make Couch Launcher my home screen?

On Couch Launcher's home screen, open the Launcher tile and choose Make it your home screen. It shows the steps for your TV. Some TVs, such as Google TV models, don't let apps replace the home screen. There you can still open Couch Launcher like any other app.

The weather is for the wrong city

Open the Launcher tile and choose Weather location. Until you pick a city, Couch Launcher uses the largest city in your TV's time zone.

The night light only works on Couch Launcher's own screens

To warm up every app, Couch Launcher needs Android's Display over other apps permission. Open the Night light tile, set Where to All apps and press OK on Allow over other apps. In the TV's settings, choose Couch Launcher and turn the permission on. On most TVs it is under Settings, Apps, Special app access, Display over other apps.

Some TVs have no such setting, and Android 10 and later don't allow it on low-memory "Go" devices. There the night light covers Couch Launcher's own screens. With a computer and adb you can still allow it once: adb shell appops set app.couchmouse.launcher SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW allow. Android hides the night light on its own Settings screens, for safety.

What does Couch Launcher send over the internet?

Only the coordinates of your weather city, to MET Norway's forecast service, and, with the online art collection on, requests for public-domain paintings from the Art Institute of Chicago. No accounts, no ads, no tracking. See the privacy policy.
