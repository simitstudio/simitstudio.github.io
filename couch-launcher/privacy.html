Couch Launcher Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy covers Couch Launcher, the home screen app for Android TV made by Simit Studio ("we"). In short: no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. To show the weather and the online art collection, your TV contacts two public services, described below. Nothing else leaves your TV.

What we collect

Nothing. We receive no information about you, your TV or how you use Couch Launcher. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

What stays on your TV

  • Your home screen settings: favourite apps, hidden apps and their order, the city you picked, and your clock, screensaver, backlight and night dimming settings.
  • The paintings downloaded for the screensaver's online art collection, with their titles and artists.
  • Whether Couch Launcher Pro is unlocked, and the day your free trial started.

Uninstalling Couch Launcher, or clearing its data in the TV's settings, removes all of this. If your TV backs up apps to your Google account, Android may include your settings in that backup (the downloaded paintings are left out). Google handles those backups.

Weather: MET Norway

The weather comes from the free forecast service of the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway, api.met.no). To get it, the TV sends the map coordinates of a city from Couch Launcher's built-in list: the city you picked, or otherwise the largest city in your TV's time zone. Couch Launcher does not use your TV's location and does not ask for location permission. The forecast is refreshed at most every 30 minutes. Sunrise and sunset times are calculated on the TV itself.

Online art: Art Institute of Chicago

With Couch Launcher Pro (or during the free trial), the screensaver can show a new set of paintings every day from the open collection of the Art Institute of Chicago (api.artic.edu). The TV asks for public-domain paintings, optionally in the art style you chose, and downloads their pictures. These requests contain nothing about you. The built-in paintings need no internet at all.

As with any website, MET Norway and the Art Institute of Chicago see your internet (IP) address when your TV connects to them. They don't receive any other information from Couch Launcher. Their own privacy policies apply to the data they handle.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: for the weather and the online art collection.
  • Run at startup: to set up night dimming again after the TV restarts (Pro, on TVs that support backlight control).
  • Google Play billing: to buy Couch Launcher Pro.
  • Your installed apps: Android lets a home screen see which apps are installed so it can show and open them. The list never leaves your TV.

Couch Launcher has no access to your location, microphone, camera, contacts or files. On TVs that share them, it lists the TV's inputs (HDMI, AV, aerial) on the TV itself, so you can switch to them.

Purchases

Couch Launcher Pro is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment and shares no payment details with us. The app only learns whether Pro is unlocked.

Children

Couch Launcher collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. For questions, email privacy@simitstudio.com or see Help.
