The app

Couch Launcher, made by Simit Studio, is a home screen app for TVs and boxes running Android TV. It is licensed to you under the Google Play Terms of Service and these terms.

Couch Launcher Pro

  • Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks the online art collection, night dimming and all screensaver styles and art themes. There is no subscription.
  • You can try Pro free for 7 days, once. After the trial ends, the free features keep working.
  • Refunds are handled by Google under Google Play's policies.

Compatibility

Couch Launcher works on most Android TV devices, but no TV maker supports it officially. Some devices, such as Google TV models, don't let apps replace the home screen. Backlight control, night dimming and the TV's inputs work only on TVs that share them with apps. Please use the free trial to check your TV before buying.

Weather, cities and paintings

Weather data comes from MET Norway and city data from GeoNames, both under CC BY 4.0. The paintings are in the public domain, from Wikimedia Commons and the Art Institute of Chicago. Forecasts can be wrong, and these online services can be unavailable at times.
