Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy covers Glow Bazaar: Lantern Sort (the "Game") for iPhone, iPad and Android, made by Simit Studio ("we"), based in Türkiye. In short: there are no accounts, your progress stays on your device, and the free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent before ads are personalised.

1. What we process

  • Advertising data (Google AdMob): your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, or Apple's IDFA only if you allow tracking on iOS), IP address (used to estimate country or city), device model, operating system, language, app version, and ad interactions such as impressions, clicks and completed rewarded ads.
  • Purchases: "Remove Ads" and the "Supporter Pack" are bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handle the payment; we receive only a confirmation and the product identifier so we can unlock and restore your item. We never see your card details.
  • Data on your device: your level, coins, unlocked styles, daily-puzzle streak and settings are stored only on your device and are not sent to us.
  • Email: if you write to us, we receive your email address and message.

The Game has no accounts, no chat, no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK.

2. Why, and on what legal basis

  • Personalised ads: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Non-personalised ads, ad measurement and fraud prevention: our legitimate interest in funding a free game, within your choices.
  • Delivering and restoring purchases: performance of our contract with you.
  • Answering support emails: your request and our legitimate interest.

3. Your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch the Game shows a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform (an IAB TCF v2.2 consent management platform). You can accept, reject or manage your choices. Ads are personalised only with your consent.
  • United States: where state law gives you the right, you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising in the privacy message.
  • You can change your choices at any time in Settings → Privacy options in the Game.
  • iOS: we ask for permission under App Tracking Transparency before the IDFA can be used. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Buying "Remove Ads" stops interstitial and banner ads. Optional rewarded ads, which you choose to watch, stay available.

4. Who we share information with

  • Google (AdMob and the User Messaging Platform) and its ad partners, as described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in the consent message.
  • Apple and Google, for app distribution and payment processing.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

We do not sell personal information for money. Under some US state laws, sharing data for targeted advertising can count as a "sale" or "sharing"; see section 3 to opt out.

5. International transfers

Our providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, transfers rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum and, for transfers from Türkiye, the safeguards in Article 9 of Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

6. Retention

Support emails are kept for up to 24 months after the matter is closed. Purchase confirmations are kept as long as needed to restore purchases and as the law requires. Advertising partners keep data under their own policies.

7. Children

The Game is made for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent.

  • EEA/UK: you can also complain to your data protection authority.
  • Türkiye (KVKK Article 11): apply to us by email; we reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the Personal Data Protection Authority.
  • California and other US states: you can ask to know, delete, correct, and opt out of sale or sharing.
  • Brazil (LGPD Article 18): confirmation, access, correction, anonymisation, portability, deletion and information about sharing.

Because the Game has no accounts, many requests are easiest through Google (myadcenter.google.com) or your device settings. Deleting the Game removes everything stored on your device.

9. Security

Data sent to our providers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). No method is 100% secure, but we keep what we hold to a minimum.

10. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date, and important changes will also be announced in the Game or on its store page. For questions or requests, email privacy@simitstudio.com.
