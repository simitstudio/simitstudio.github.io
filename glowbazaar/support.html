Help

Answers to common questions. Still stuck? Email support@simitstudio.com and tell us your device and level number.

How do I play?

Tap a lantern to lift its top beads, then tap another lantern to drop them. Beads can go onto an empty lantern or onto a bead of the same colour, if there is room. Fill a lantern with four beads of one colour to light it. Light every lantern to finish the level. Each lantern you light plays the next note of the level's melody.

What are the dark "?" beads?

Hidden beads appear from level 16. Their colour shows when they become the top bead of their lantern.

I'm stuck

Use Undo (3 free per level), add an Extra lantern, or tap Restart. Every level can be solved.

Can I play offline?

Yes. The whole game works without internet. Ads and purchases need a connection.

Remove Ads and restoring purchases

"Remove Ads" removes interstitial and banner ads; optional rewarded ads stay available if you want the bonuses. On a new device, open Settings → Restore purchases while signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account.

Changing ad and privacy choices

Open Settings → Privacy options. On iPhone and iPad you can also change tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See the Privacy Policy.

Colour-blind friendly mode

Turn on Settings → Colour symbols to show a small symbol on every bead.

My progress disappeared

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the game or clearing its data removes it. Purchases can always be restored.
