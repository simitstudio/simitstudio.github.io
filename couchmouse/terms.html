CouchMouse Pro

  • Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks the mouse cursor, typing into apps and app shortcuts on all the TVs you use with the app. There is no subscription.
  • A 7-day free trial may be offered once. After it ends, the free remote features keep working.
  • On the App Store, Pro can be shared with your family through Family Sharing.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies.

Compatibility

CouchMouse works with most Android TV and Google TV devices, but no TV maker supports it officially. Some TV apps ignore touch input, and some TVs limit accessibility services. Please use the free trial to check that it works with your TV before buying.
