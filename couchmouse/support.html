Help

Answers to common questions. Still stuck? Email support@simitstudio.com or ask on GitHub.

The app doesn't find my TV

Check that the TV is on, and that the phone and the TV are on the same Wi-Fi network. Some routers keep the 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz networks apart, or have "guest" or "AP isolation" settings that block devices from seeing each other.

  • On iPhone, allow CouchMouse in Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network.
  • You can always type the TV's address instead: tap Enter TV address. The address is shown in CouchMouse TV, or in the TV's settings under Network.
Which TVs work?

TVs and streaming boxes with Android TV or Google TV. Examples are Sony, TCL, Philips, Xiaomi, Hisense Android models, Chromecast with Google TV, Google TV Streamer and NVIDIA Shield. The free remote uses the TV's built-in remote service. The mouse cursor needs CouchMouse TV on the TV, running Android 7 or later. Amazon Fire TV is not supported yet.

How do I get the mouse cursor?
  1. Install CouchMouse TV on your TV: open the Play Store on the TV and search for "CouchMouse TV". When the remote is connected, the phone app can open that page on the TV for you.
  2. Open CouchMouse TV on the TV, choose Agree and turn on, then select CouchMouse TV and turn it on.
  3. In the phone app, open the Touchpad tab and pair. You can scan the QR code on the TV (in the app, or with your phone's camera), or type the 4-digit code it shows.
The TV says "Restricted setting"

Android 13 and later protect accessibility settings for apps that weren't installed from a store. Open the TV's Settings → Apps → CouchMouse TV, choose Allow restricted settings, then turn the service on again. Installing CouchMouse TV from Google Play avoids this.

Taps don't work in one app

A few TV apps ignore touch input. In those apps, use the arrows on the Remote tab. They work everywhere.

Typing doesn't appear on the TV

First select the text box on the TV, using the cursor or the arrows. When CouchMouse TV sees a text box, the app shows "The TV is waiting for text". Some apps use their own on-screen keyboard and ignore typed text. Use that app's keyboard with the cursor instead.

Is it safe? What does the accessibility permission allow?

CouchMouse TV acts only on commands from phones you paired with a code or QR code shown on your TV. Everything stays on your home network, the connection is encrypted, and nothing is collected. The TV app is open source, so anyone can check it. See the privacy policy.

Restoring CouchMouse Pro

On a new phone, open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchase, using the same Apple ID or Google account you bought Pro with.

Can I try CouchMouse without a TV?

Yes. On the list of TVs, tap Try without a TV. A simulated TV appears above the controls and shows what the remote, touchpad and keyboard do.
