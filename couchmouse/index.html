A real mouse for your Android TV

Your phone becomes a touchpad, keyboard and remote for Android TV and Google TV. Point, tap and scroll anywhere, even in apps that were never made for a remote.

Get CouchMouse How it works

Free remote · Pro is a one-time purchase · No subscription, no ads, no tracking

Real cursor

Move a mouse pointer across the TV with your thumb. Tap to click, hold to long-press, two fingers to scroll.

Type with your phone

Search, log in and fill in boxes with your phone's keyboard instead of the on-screen one.

Full remote

Arrows, OK, back, home, volume and play/pause, free. It works even without the TV app.

Private by design

Everything stays on your home Wi-Fi. Only phones you pair with a code shown on the TV can connect.

How it works

  1. Install CouchMouse on your iPhone or Android phone. The phone and TV must be on the same Wi-Fi.
  2. Pick your TV in the app and type the code your TV shows. The remote works right away. No TV nearby? Tap Try without a TV to see it working on a simulated one.
  3. For the mouse cursor, install the free CouchMouse TV app on your TV. The phone app can open its Play Store page on the TV for you. Then allow it in the TV's accessibility settings, as its setup screen explains.
