Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy covers the CouchMouse phone apps for iPhone and Android, and the CouchMouse TV app for Android TV and Google TV. They are made by Simit Studio ("we"). In short: we don't collect any personal data. The apps talk only to your own TV over your home network.

What we collect

Nothing. CouchMouse has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It contains no third-party data-collection tools. We receive no information about you or how you use the apps.

What stays on your devices

  • On your phone: the TVs you paired (their name and network address) and the keys and certificate details that let them recognise each other. The keys are kept in the phone's secure storage (iOS Keychain or the Android Keystore). Your settings are kept there too, such as touchpad speed.
  • On your TV: the paired phones (a name such as "iPhone", and a one-way hash of each phone's key) and the TV's own encryption key, which never leaves the TV.

Deleting the app removes this data. You can also forget a TV in the phone app, or a phone in CouchMouse TV, at any time.

Network

The phone connects directly to your TV on your local network. These connections are encrypted. Nothing is sent to the internet by CouchMouse.

Permissions

  • Local network (phone): to find your TV and control it.
  • Camera (phone, optional): only to scan the pairing QR code shown on your TV. Images are not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Accessibility service (TV): CouchMouse TV uses Android's accessibility service to show the mouse cursor and to tap, scroll and type for you. It acts only on commands from phones you have paired. It reads the screen only to find the text box you are typing into. It never collects, stores or shares what is on your screen.

Purchases

CouchMouse Pro is bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handle the payment and share no payment details with us. The app only learns whether Pro is unlocked.

Children

CouchMouse collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. For questions, email privacy@simitstudio.com.
